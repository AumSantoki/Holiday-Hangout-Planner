Feature Architecture:

  • Public Holiday Calendar: Fetches verified national public holidays by country and calendar year.
  • Activity Engine: Recommends dynamic hangout activities tailored by group vibe and participant count.
  • QR Code Generator: Encodes shareable invitation URLs into responsive QR codes for mobile scanning.
  • Cloud Database: Stores hangout invitations, notes, and powers the community hangout wall.
To connect your own database, set DATABASE_URL=postgresql://... in .env and restart the server.
